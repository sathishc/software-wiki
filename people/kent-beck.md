# Kent Beck

Author of *Extreme Programming Explained* (1999) and a principal originator of
**Agile** software. With Martin Fowler he authored *Refactoring*; with
Ward Cunningham he helped codify **Test-Driven Development**. Beck is one of the
two or three most consequential forces in how modern software is *built day to
day*.

## Core contribution

- **Extreme Programming (XP)** — a lightweight methodology that takes good
  practices (pairing, short iterations, on-site customer) to the *extreme*:
  continuous integration, test-first development, refactoring, week-long
  releases. XP is the origin of [Test-Driven Development (TDD)](../concepts/tdd.md).
- **JUnit** — the Java unit-testing framework (with Erich Gamma) that made
  [Unit Testing](../concepts/unit-testing.md) a first-class, automated practice in the JVM world.
- **Simplicity** — a recurring theme: do the *simplest* thing that works, now,
  and improve as you learn. This is the intellectual ancestor of [You Aren't Gonna Need It (YAGNI)](../concepts/yagni.md) and
  [Keep It Simple (KISS)](../concepts/kiss.md).

## His frame

Beck's consistent argument: **feedback is everything.** The whole XP loop —
small releases, fast tests, constant refactoring, pair programming — is one
machine for *shrinking the feedback loop* between "I made a change" and "I know
whether it was a good one." That same feedback logic reappears at every scale
in this wiki: from a [Test-Driven Development (TDD)](../concepts/tdd.md) loop, to a [Refactoring](../concepts/refactoring.md) loop, to a [Continuous Integration & Continuous Delivery (CI/CD)](../concepts/ci-cd.md)
pipeline, to [DORA Metrics](../concepts/dora-metrics.md).

## Where he fits

Beck is the **process & feedback** anchor. He links [Test-Driven Development (TDD)](../concepts/tdd.md) → [Refactoring](../concepts/refactoring.md)
→ [Continuous Integration & Continuous Delivery (CI/CD)](../concepts/ci-cd.md) into one coherent story about fast feedback. He is also the
individual-to-team bridge: [Test-Driven Development (TDD)](../concepts/tdd.md) is the solo craft, [Code Review](../concepts/code-review.md) is the
team version of the same idea.
