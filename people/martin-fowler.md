# Martin Fowler

Author of *Refactoring* (2001/2018), *Patterns of Enterprise Application
Architecture* (2002), and the ubiquitous `martinfowler.com` — the single most
cited practical voice in modern software design. His work turned a collection
of scattered ideas into *named, reusable, teachable* patterns.

## Core contribution

Fowler's lasting gift is **giving names to problems and solutions**:
[Refactoring](../concepts/refactoring.md) and its catalogue of smells, the **MVC** pattern,
[Domain-Driven Design (DDD)](../concepts/domain-driven-design.md)'s [Ubiquitous Language](../concepts/ubiquitous-language.md) and [Bounded Context](../concepts/bounded-context.md)
(bliki essays), and the definitions of [Continuous Integration & Continuous Delivery (CI/CD)](../concepts/ci-cd.md). His recurring stance: **a
well-formed name is worth more than a clever trick**, because it makes a known
pattern *communicable*.

## His frame, in his own recurring words

> "Refactoring is the process of changing a program in such a way that it does
> not alter the program's external behaviour while improving its internal
> structure."

> On DDD: the model is the *heart* of the software; get the ubiquitous
> language right and the design follows.

## Where he fits in this wiki

Fowler is the connective tissue: [Refactoring](../concepts/refactoring.md) links to his work directly,
[Continuous Integration & Continuous Delivery (CI/CD)](../concepts/ci-cd.md) and [DORA Metrics](../concepts/dora-metrics.md) trace to his bliki and *Accelerate*, and
[Domain-Driven Design (DDD)](../concepts/domain-driven-design.md) is his bliki's most-quoted contribution. He is the
**"how do you actually do this"** counterweight to the purely theoretical
[Architecture & Design](../pages/architecture.md) debates in this wiki.
